# v1.0.51 — Zone 1 Factor Closure v1

## Purpose

Reduce Zone 1 plant-factor uncertainty using direct residential-landscape evidence without expanding watering authority.

## Evidence admission

- Direct UC residential landscape Plant Factor guidance is authoritative for commissioned plant-type categories when the category mapping is explicit.
- Agricultural crop coefficients remain supporting context and cannot directly authorize a landscape Plant Factor.
- Source-supported baseline PF and establishment-stage irrigation management are separate questions.
- The unidentified drought-tolerant ornamental group remains unresolved rather than being forced into the desert-adapted PF category.

## Mixed-zone rule

The zone may select the highest admitted PF only when every controlling plant group has an admitted factor. If any controlling group remains factor-unresolved, the zone remains partially resolved and no authoritative zone PF is emitted.

## Density / geometry

Plant Factor v2 does not synthesize a density coefficient. Projected canopy or planted area is a separate geometry/delivery input for future quantitative water-volume calculations.

## Safety boundary

This milestone is advisory only. It does not alter scheduling, runtime, Rachio command paths, retries, execution authorization, or live-control authorization.
