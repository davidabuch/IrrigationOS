# v1.0.50 Landscape Factor Resolution v1

This milestone adds a deterministic, advisory factor-resolution layer for the commissioned Zone 1 mixed hydrozone.

## Scientific boundary

Evidence is classified by directness: landscape plant factor, urban horticulture guidance, agricultural crop coefficient, qualitative horticulture, inference, or unknown. Agricultural crop coefficients may inform review but cannot directly authorize a residential landscape plant factor. Numeric ranges are preserved; the resolver does not synthesize midpoints.

Incidental mature palms are excluded from zone demand. Establishing citrus remains unresolved until an admitted establishment policy exists. Plant-health observations remain separate evidence and cannot inflate plant factors or authorize runtime changes.

The current Zone 1 result is intentionally `partially_resolved`: Podocarpus has directly admissible established-landscape woody-plant guidance, while Peruvian lilies, citrus, fig, passion fruit, and the taxonomically mixed drought-tolerant group remain insufficient for an authoritative mixed-zone factor. Sparse-canopy density is also unresolved.

## Safety boundary

This milestone does not change quantitative water-balance inputs, runtime, schedules, execution, Rachio control, supervised operation, canary behavior, or authorization. `execution_authorized` and `live_control_authorized` remain false.
